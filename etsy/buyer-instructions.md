# Buyer instructions

Put this on a single page in Canva, add your template link, download it as a PDF, and upload that PDF as the Etsy file. Use the same forest (`#1E3A32`) and cream (`#F7F4EE`) as the invoice so the download matches the listing.

---

Your invoice template is ready.

Open this link on a computer, while you are signed in to Canva:

PASTE YOUR CANVA TEMPLATE LINK HERE

Canva will ask you to use the template. That makes your own copy. A free Canva account is enough.

Then:

1. Click the business name and type yours. Change the letter in the circle to your initial.
2. Replace the client, the dates, and the invoice number.
3. Click each line item and type your services. Two rows are blank. Duplicate a row if you need more.
4. Update the subtotal, tax, and total. Change DUE to PAID when the invoice is settled.
5. Download as PDF Print to email or print. PNG and JPG are there if you want an image.

Fonts are Playfair Display and Montserrat. Both are free in Canva. If a font looks different after you open the file, select the text and choose that font again.

The page size is set (US Letter or A4) and should stay as it is.

You can send the finished invoice to your clients. Please don’t resell or share the template link.

Questions about editing: reply to your Etsy order and I will help.
