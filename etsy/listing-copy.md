Editable Invoice Template Canva, Small Business Invoice, Modern Printable Invoice Form, US Letter and A4

## Description

A clean, editable invoice for freelancers and small businesses. Change the words, the colors, and the logo letter in Canva, then download a PDF to send or print.

This is a digital template. Nothing is shipped.

WHAT’S INCLUDED
• Invoice, US Letter (8.5 × 11 in)
• Invoice, A4 (210 × 297 mm)
• Canva template link, sent in a one-page PDF
• Short editing steps

WHAT YOU CAN EDIT
• Business name, tagline, and the letter in the logo mark
• Your client’s name and address
• Invoice number, issue date, due date, and the Due / Paid status
• Every line item, quantity, rate, and amount
• Notes, payment methods, tax, discount, and the total
• Colors and fonts
• Add or duplicate rows if you need more lines

HOW IT WORKS
1. After checkout, download the PDF from Etsy.
2. Open the Canva link and choose “Use template” so you get your own copy. A free Canva account is enough.
3. Replace the sample business, client, and prices.
4. Download as PDF Print to send or print. PNG and JPG work if you only need a picture.

PLEASE NOTE
• Edit on a laptop or desktop. A phone can change text, but the layout is easier on a larger screen.
• This is not a Word or Google Docs file. The page size stays US Letter or A4.
• The sample names and prices in the photos are placeholders.
• Colors can shift slightly between screens and printers.

USE
Personal use for your own business. You can send the finished invoice to your clients. Don’t resell, share, or give away the template file or the Canva link.
